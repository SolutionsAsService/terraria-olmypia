# Olympia for Terraria: design and roadmap

## Progression

| Stage | Content |
|---|---|
| Pre-Hardmode | Find the **Temple of Olympus** (surface) and the **Labyrinth of the Minotaur** (underground; a roped shaft leads in). Craft or find the **Horn of the Labyrinth**; fight **the Minotaur** in the open. Aegis, Labrys, Bronze Xiphos. Bronze Bars make the Xiphos and Bronze Plate; Ambrosia Flowers make the potions. |
| Hardmode | Craft the **Mirror of Perseus** (8 Gorgon Scales + 6 Hallowed Bars). Fight **Medusa**. Gorgon's Eye, Bow of Artemis. |
| Post-Plantera | Craft the **Offering to Zeus** (10 Lightning Fragments + 10 Hallowed Bars + 5 Gorgon Scales). Fight **Zeus**. Cloak of Olympus, Thunderbolt, Trident of Poseidon. |

## Next (not built)

* **The Hydra** (a worm boss), **Cerberus**, **Poseidon** and **Hades** (an underworld temple); **Pegasus** and **chariot** mounts.
* Armour sets (the Hoplite, Spartan and Olympian sets) once the equip sheets can be drawn on the player template.
* A **Town NPC** (an oracle who sells maps of the labyrinth), a **Delphi** biome with an oracle chamber, olive groves and amphorae, custom marble walls and furniture, music and trophies.
* Multiplayer checks and balance from real play.
